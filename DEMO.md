## Questions you should be ready for

* *Why didn't randomization help?* Candidates, none tested: the stress levels are mild and nominal policies already tolerate them; the weak-motor effect only appears near saturation; the policy is feedforward and the hidden gains are unobservable; seed variance (std about 4-12 pp across seeds) exceeds any effect at this scale. The discriminating experiment: longer training or a recurrent policy, evaluated on a **newly generated** test set.
* *Did you tune on the test set?* No: the test split was evaluated exactly twice, both pre-declared (final checkpoints, and the ~500k checkpoints), logged in `results/test_access_log.jsonl`. Reward scale and discount were chosen on validation data.
* *Is this reinforcement learning "from raw sensors"?* No. Goal location and velocities are privileged inputs; say so first.
* *What did the AI do versus you?* The implementation was AI-generated; the experimental decisions you add (see README, section "What you need to be able to explain") should be named as yours.
