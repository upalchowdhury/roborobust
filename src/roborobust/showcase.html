03 / Engineering that matters

Wheel–ground contact drives motion in MuJoCo. A verifier independent of reward checks contact-free arrival and stopping. Frozen layouts, paired noise streams, five training seeds, checkpoint hashes, and raw traces make the comparison auditable.

04 / Takeaway

Domain randomization showed no detectable advantage at this budget. The reactive baseline performs better. The strongest engineering story is diagnosing reward-driven idling on validation, fixing the training protocol, and reporting a negative result honestly.
